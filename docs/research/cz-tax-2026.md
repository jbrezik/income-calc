# CZ tax and insurance parameters 2026 (with legislated 2027 changes)

Researched 2026-10-11. Primary sources preferred (zakonyprolidi.cz consolidated ZDP version 157,
valid 2026-08-01 to 2026-12-31; cssz.gov.cz; vzp.cz; mpsv.gov.cz; financnisprava.gov.cz;
psp.cz). Secondary sites only where noted.

Key 2026 change mid-year: zákon 90/2026 Sb. (novela 589/1992 Sb., effective 2026-07-01) cut the
OSVČ minimum social assessment base from 40 % back to 35 % of average wage. Applied to the whole
year 2026 (Jan-Jun overpayments settled in the 2026 přehled / refundable). Use the 35 % figures
for 2026 annual calculations.

## Uncertainties

- 90/2026 Sb. law number and "retroactive" framing: CSSZ says the new 5 005 Kč minimum applies to advances from July, with Jan-Jun recalculated at 35 % in the 2026 přehled (effectively whole-year 35 %). Law number only from secondary sources (podnikatel.cz, money.cz).
- DPS 1 700 Kč/month threshold: law text references "amount giving max state contribution", figure from secondary sources.
- Mortgage 300 000 Kč transitional rule: GFŘ info + 386/2020 Sb. čl. IV; wording criticised as unclear (dauc.cz). Edge cases (refinancing, acquisition near 2021-01-01) need case check.
- 2027 credit amounts (student 4 020, školkovné cap, EET credit 5 000) taken from the bill as of first reading; final text of 180/2026 Sb. not read (PDF unparseable). Confirm against Sbírka.
- 2027 paušál Band I social part 6 074 Kč: my own rounding of 18 083 x 1,15 x 29,2 % gives 6 073 Kč; figure from banky.cz, not yet on financnisprava.gov.cz.
- Proposed doubling of the no-return income thresholds from 2027 (businessinfo.cz): legislative status unconfirmed.
- VAT obrat composition (whether reverse-charge EU services count toward 2 M) not verified against ZDPH text; under the 2025 rules obrat counts supplies with place of supply in CZ.
- IT activity classification: most IT freelance is volná živnost (60 %); income from licensing own software as author's work may be § 7 odst. 1 písm. c) (40 %). Depends on contract.
